Why We Never Click Your Links

Traditional scanners open links in a browser to see what loads. This creates serious dangers:

  • Drive-by Viruses: Simply opening a malicious page can infect devices with malware.
  • Attacker Tracking: Clicking links with tracking codes lets scammers know your email or phone number is active.
  • Sneaky Pages: Attackers often show innocent pages to scanners and malicious pages to real people.

PhishGuard operates on a strict Zero-Execution guarantee: we analyze links entirely by inspecting their structure, so your device remains 100% safe.
